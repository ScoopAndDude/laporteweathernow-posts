# laporteweathernow-posts
Daily Scoop posts for laporteweathernow.com. Every post and edit is saved here with its time.

- `scoop-posts.json`: the Daily Scoop posts, newest first (laporteweathernow.com/daily-scoop).
- `nws-snapshot.json`: the official NWS, SPC and WPC weather each morning's post is written from, saved by the "NWS weather snapshot" job (clock times already in La Porte's Central time).
- `scorecard.json`: every Scoop forecast graded against the La Porte airport (same rules as the Track Record page), saved with the snapshot. The homepage's "How we've done" line reads it.
- Second opinions (added Oct. 2, 2026, `scripts/consensus.py`, run by the snapshot job):
  - `nws-snapshot.json` → `consensus`: the NWS forecast next to four computer models from Open-Meteo (free, CC BY 4.0): NBM, HRRR, GFS and ECMWF. `blend` = the middle value of NWS and the models; `adjusted` = blend corrected by our own average past error at the La Porte airport (`scorecard.json` → `bias`) once 14+ days are graded, capped at 3 degrees. `suggested` is what the Daily Scoop's forecasts use. Rain chances stay NWS's.
  - `nws-snapshot.json` → `workdayRain`: NWS hour-by-hour rain chances for today's and tomorrow's work hours (6 a.m.-6 p.m.) in plain words.
  - `morning-log.json`: what NWS and the blend said for the same day at about 5 a.m. (when Hard Hat Weather makes its calls), saved once a day and never changed. `scorecard.json` → `morning` grades it.
- `health.json`: the daily site health check (pages, files, version stamps, data freshness, on-time Scoops, failed jobs), saved by the "Site health check" job. `problems` lists everything; `urgent` lists what gets emailed.
- `markets-data.json`: BLS jobs and prices for laporteweathernow.com/markets, saved twice a day by the "Markets numbers from BLS" job.
- `world-watch.json`: El Niño status and climate facts for laporteweathernow.com/disasters, updated weekly.
- `amazon-watch.json`: numbers, news and new species for the Amazon Rainforest Watch page (laporteweathernow.com/amazon): deforestation, illegal gold mining, drought and fire, from official data as reported by trusted outlets, with sources. Updated weekly.
- `fuel-prices.json` (added Oct. 1, 2026): this week's official average gas and diesel prices by region, a few states and cities, from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, for The Chase (laporteweathernow.com/camp-map). Saved by the "Fuel prices from EIA" job (`scripts/fuel_prices.py`) only when EIA has a new week.
- `tiles/` (added Oct. 1, 2026): The Chase's map squares: campsites, rest areas, dog parks, springs, water, dump stations, showers, laundry, libraries, propane and truck stops for the whole U.S. from OpenStreetMap, one small file per 1-degree square, so the map loads in about half a second instead of waiting on OpenStreetMap's volunteer servers. Rebuilt every Monday by the "Camp map places from OpenStreetMap" job (`scripts/places_build.py`): one runner per state, then a merge. Branch `places-parts` keeps each state's latest copy, so a state that fails to download keeps last week's. Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Don't edit by hand.
- `preview/`: pages waiting for the next site publish, for Scoop to look at (not linked, not indexed). Removed once they're live (empty right now).
- Branch `live`, file `home.json`: La Porte's latest NWS reading and forecast, replaced every 15 minutes by the "Homepage weather copy" job. The homepage shows it (with its time) when NWS is slow or down.
- Backup Daily Scoop: if the morning Scoop isn't up by 6:30 AM Central, the "Backup Daily Scoop" job posts the official NWS forecast in its place, marked as automatic (`"auto": true`, no forecasts on record). The morning Scoop task replaces it when its write-up comes in.
- Email alerts: when the daily site check finds something readers would notice (`urgent` in health.json), the job fails on purpose so GitHub emails Scoop.
- Weather radio (added Sept. 30, 2026): every 15 minutes the "Weather radio" job (`scripts/radio_broadcast.py`) writes the La Porte County broadcast from the newest NWS alerts, La Porte airport reading, NWS forecast and today's Daily Scoop, in words made for listening. When the words change, Kokoro-82M (a free, open-source voice, Apache 2.0) records it. Branch `radio` keeps only the newest recording (`laporte-<hash>.mp3`) and `radio.json` (time, length, parts, read-along text); the Alerts page's radio player plays it. Run it by hand from Actions > Weather radio: "force" records even if nothing changed, "check" also runs speech-to-text on the recording and saves the result as `check.txt` on the `radio` branch.
- Timer (added Sept. 30, 2026): GitHub's own schedules for this repository have been hours late or missing, so an outside clock (a Google Apps Script trigger, "LPWN timer", in the business Google account) sends a `tick` (repository_dispatch) every 15 minutes. The "Timer" job (`scripts/timer.py`) then starts what's due in La Porte time: the homepage copy and the weather radio every tick, the NWS snapshot at 5:00, the backup Scoop 6:30-8:09, the site check at 8:10, BLS markets at 9:15 and 4:45, The Chase's map squares on Mondays (3:15 AM, with a 3:15 PM second chance that only builds if the first didn't), and fuel prices Tuesday 5:45 PM and Wednesday and Thursday 9:45 AM. The jobs keep their GitHub schedules as a second chance.
- Guard rails (Sept. 30, 2026):
  - `tools/check_site.py SITE.zip`: run before every publish; it must say PASS (links, version stamps, menus, sitemap, and every page opened in English and Darja on a phone-sized screen with no script errors or sideways scrolling).
  - The daily site check lists `urgent` (readers would notice) and `stuck` (a job stopped) in health.json. Only the daily run started by the timer emails Scoop (it fails on purpose); checks run by hand just report.
  - `tools/lpwn-timer.gs`: the Google Apps Script that sends the timer's ticks and, once a day, emails if a job is stuck. Setup steps are at the top of the file.
