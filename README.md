# laporteweathernow-posts
Daily Scoop posts for laporteweathernow.com. Every post and edit is saved here with its time.

- `scoop-posts.json`: the Daily Scoop posts, newest first (laporteweathernow.com/daily-scoop).
- `nws-snapshot.json`: the official NWS, SPC and WPC weather each morning's post is written from, saved by the "NWS weather snapshot" job (clock times already in La Porte's Central time).
- `scorecard.json`: every Scoop forecast graded against the La Porte airport (same rules as the Track Record page), saved with the snapshot. The homepage's "How we've done" line reads it.
- Second opinions (added Oct. 2, 2026, `scripts/consensus.py`, run by the snapshot job):
  - `nws-snapshot.json` → `consensus`: the NWS forecast next to four computer models from Open-Meteo (free, CC BY 4.0): NBM, HRRR, GFS and ECMWF. `blend` = the middle value of NWS and the models; `adjusted` = blend corrected by our own average past error at the La Porte airport (`scorecard.json` → `bias`) once 14+ days are graded, capped at 3 degrees. `suggested` is what the Daily Scoop's forecasts use. Rain chances stay NWS's.
  - `nws-snapshot.json` → `workdayRain`: NWS hour-by-hour rain chances for today's and tomorrow's work hours (6 a.m.-6 p.m.) in plain words.
  - `morning-log.json`: what NWS and the blend said for the same day at about 5 a.m. (when Hard Hat Weather makes its calls), saved once a day and never changed. `scorecard.json` → `morning` grades it.
- `health.json`: the daily site health check (pages, files, version stamps, data freshness, on-time Scoops, failed jobs), saved by the "Site health check" job. `problems` lists everything; `urgent` lists what gets emailed.
- `markets-data.json`: BLS jobs and prices for scoopanddude.com/markets/ (moved from laporteweathernow.com/markets on Oct. 7, 2026), saved twice a day by the "Markets numbers from BLS" job.
- `world-watch.json`: El Niño status and climate facts for laporteweathernow.com/disasters, updated weekly.
- `amazon-watch.json`: numbers, news and new species for the Amazon Rainforest Watch page (scoopanddude.com/amazon/, moved from laporteweathernow.com/amazon on Oct. 7, 2026): deforestation, illegal gold mining, drought and fire, from official data as reported by trusted outlets, with sources. Updated weekly.
- `fuel-prices.json` (added Oct. 1, 2026): this week's official average gas and diesel prices by region, a few states and cities, from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, for the camp map (scoopanddude.com/camp-map/, moved from laporteweathernow.com/camp-map on Oct. 7, 2026). Saved by the "Fuel prices from EIA" job (`scripts/fuel_prices.py`) only when EIA has a new week.
- Branch `space` (added Oct. 4, 2026), files `fires.json`, `steady.json`, `satellites.json` and `status.json` (what the newest run did): "Earth from space" on laporteweathernow.com/disasters. Fires NASA's VIIRS cameras spotted in the last 24 hours (NASA FIRMS' free global files, NOAA-20, NOAA-21 and Suomi NPP; low-confidence detections left out; merged into 0.1-degree squares; `steady.json`, rebuilt daily from NOAA-20's 7-day file, marks squares with heat on 5+ of the last 7 days, which are almost always steel mills, refineries, gas flares or volcanoes, so the page shows those in gray instead of calling them fires) and the orbits of CelesTrak's weather satellites plus the space station, which the page turns into live positions. Saved every 3 hours by the "Earth from space data" job (`scripts/space_data.py`); only the newest copy is kept, and a source that doesn't answer keeps its last good file. Credit: NASA FIRMS, CelesTrak.
- `tiles/` (added Oct. 1, 2026): the camp map's squares (scoopanddude.com/camp-map/; The Chase on laporteweathernow.com until Oct. 7, 2026): campsites, rest areas, dog parks, springs, water, dump stations, showers, laundry, libraries, propane and truck stops for the whole U.S. from OpenStreetMap, one small file per 1-degree square, so the map loads in about half a second instead of waiting on OpenStreetMap's volunteer servers. Rebuilt every Monday by the "Camp map places from OpenStreetMap" job (`scripts/places_build.py`): one runner per state, then a merge. Branch `places-parts` keeps each state's latest copy, so a state that fails to download keeps last week's. Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Don't edit by hand.
- `preview/`: pages waiting for the next site publish, for Scoop to look at (not linked, not indexed). Removed once they're live (empty right now).
- Branch `live`, file `home.json`: La Porte's latest NWS reading and forecast, replaced every 15 minutes by the "Homepage weather copy" job. The homepage shows it (with its time) when NWS is slow or down.
- Backup Daily Scoop: if the morning Scoop isn't up by 6:30 AM Central, the "Backup Daily Scoop" job posts the official NWS forecast in its place, marked as automatic (`"auto": true`, no forecasts on record). The morning Scoop task replaces it when its write-up comes in.
- Daily Scoop updates (added Oct. 2, 2026, `scripts/scoop_update.py`, job "Daily Scoop updates", started by the Timer every 15 minutes): keeps today's Scoop current without AI or a site publish, using only the NWS forecast, NWS alerts for La Porte County and the La Porte airport, in fixed sentences.
  - A written Scoop gets one paragraph at the top of its body, "Automatic update, 3:45 p.m.: ...", replaced each time, when an alert for La Porte County starts, changes or ends (right away), NWS changes today's, tonight's or tomorrow's forecast by 3+ degrees or 20+ points of rain chance compared with the forecast the Scoop was written from, or the airport reads 5+ degrees off NWS's earlier hour-by-hour forecast or reports rain or snow in an hour forecast at 20% or less. Apart from alerts, at most once every 55 minutes.
  - The automatic backup post is rebuilt with NWS's newest forecast, reading and alerts: right away when alerts change, otherwise hourly when NWS has something newer. It keeps `"auto": true`, so the morning Scoop task still replaces it.
  - `posted`, `forecasts` and `corrections` never change (the Track Record stays honest); `updated` shows the newest change. If NWS's forecast or any of its alert lists don't answer, nothing changes that round.
  - Alert end times are NWS's own `ends`. An alert that drops off NWS's list before its end time is kept for one more check, since NWS's list sometimes skips an alert while it's being updated; still gone 15 minutes later (or canceled), it's reported as ended.
  - `scoop-updates.json`: the job's notes for today (the forecast the Scoop was written from, what was last published, the alerts last seen, today's updates). Safe to delete; the job starts over from the current post. Run the job by hand from Actions > Daily Scoop updates.
- `scoop/` (added Oct. 6, 2026): one plain web page per Daily Scoop (`scoop/<date>.html`, weekly outlooks `scoop/week-of-<date>.html`), `latest.html` (a copy of the newest written Scoop), `index.html` (every Scoop by date) and `sitemap.xml`, so search engines can find each Scoop. Built from `scoop-posts.json` by the "Daily Scoop pages" job (`scripts/scoop_pages.py`), started by the Timer every tick and whenever the posts change; it only saves pages that changed. laporteweathernow.com shows them at `/scoop/<date>`, `/scoop/latest`, `/scoop` and `/scoop-sitemap.xml` through proxy rules in the site's `_redirects`, so new Scoops never need a site publish. Written Scoops get Article data with Scoop as the author; automatic backup posts get a page marked noindex and stay out of the sitemap. New written Scoops are announced to Bing and other IndexNow search engines (the key file is on the site). `scoop/template.html` is the site's header, menu and footer: the site's builder (`tools/oct-6-seo/build.py` in the site repository) writes it, so rebuild it when the menu changes. Don't edit the pages by hand.
- `snow.json` (added Oct. 6, 2026): every National Weather Service snow report for La Porte County (local storm reports from NWS Northern Indiana, as archived by the Iowa Environmental Mesonet at Iowa State), grouped into storms, this season and last, for laporteweathernow.com/snow-totals. Saved by the "Snow totals" job (`scripts/snow_totals.py`), started by the Timer every tick; it asks for new reports once an hour October through May and once a day in summer, and saves only when the reports change (or every 6 hours, to show they were checked). Run it by hand from Actions > Snow totals with "force".
- `shore.json` (added Oct. 6, 2026): the Shore Call, La Porte Weather Now's morning call for the La Porte County shore (Washington Park, Long Beach, Mount Baldy, the Michigan City harbor): swim (beach season), piers, small boats with their best hours, and from mid-October through April the lake-effect snow band (towns, school districts and main roads), made by fixed rules from NWS Northern Indiana's beach and nearshore marine forecasts, its forecast grid and NWS alerts, for laporteweathernow.com/lake-michigan. Each call is graded the next morning against the Michigan City buoy (NDBC 45170), the Michigan City lighthouse station (MCYI3) and, for snow, the reports in `snow.json`; `score` counts the graded calls made before 9 a.m. The rules are at the top of `scripts/shore_call.py`. `shore-today.json` is just the newest call, small, for the home page's Shore Call chip. Saved by the "Shore Call" job, started by the Timer at 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 AM; run it by hand from Actions > Shore Call with "force" (a call made after 9 a.m. is shown but not scored).
- `school.json` and `school-outcomes.txt` (added Oct. 6, 2026): the School Delay Watch's record. The watch on the home page says "delays unlikely", "delay possible" or "closing possible" for the next school morning, from the NWS forecast for La Porte; it never says a school is closed. The "School Delay Watch record" job (`scripts/school_call.py`) saves the call at 8 p.m. the night before each school day (Timer, Sunday-Thursday 8:00 PM, 9:00 PM as a second chance) and grades it against `school-outcomes.txt`, a hand-kept list of what the six districts did (one line per morning; the format is at the top of the file). Log every morning the watch flagged and every morning a district delayed or closed; the job grades a morning within a minute of the file changing.
- `race.json` (added Oct. 6, 2026): the Forecast Race for laporteweathernow.com/forecast-race. Each morning it saves what five forecasters say about tomorrow at the La Porte airport (KPPO): the National Weather Service's official forecast (its NDFD forecast grids as they stood at 5:30 AM, from NOAA Open Data on AWS), NOAA's AI model (AIGFS, in use since Dec. 17, 2025) and its regular GFS (00 UTC runs, NOAA NOMADS), and ECMWF's AI (AIFS) and regular (IFS) models (00 UTC runs, ECMWF open data, CC BY 4.0). It saves the temperature at 06, 12, 18 and 00 UTC (1 AM, 7 AM, 1 PM, 7 PM in summer time) and the 24-hour rain total (06 to 06 UTC), then grades them the next morning against KPPO (Iowa Environmental Mesonet) and keeps the leaderboard (average miss in degrees, share within 3 degrees, rain calls, days won; a racer is ranked once it has at least half as many graded days as the leader). Saved forecasts never change; a racer that doesn't answer isn't scored that day. The forecasts made Sept. 27 to Oct. 5 were filled in on Oct. 6 from the same public archives (marked `backfilled`; Europe's models only from Oct. 4, as ECMWF keeps a few days). Job "Forecast Race" (`scripts/forecast_race.py`), started by the Timer at 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 AM; run it by hand from Actions > Forecast Race (mode force, or backfill with from/to dates).
- `devotion.json` and `devotion/` (added Oct. 6, 2026): the Sky Devotion, a short devotion picked each morning by what the sky is doing. `devotion/library.json` holds the 78 devotions (written by La Porte Weather Now, one per kind of sky: clear, a beautiful day, gray, rain, storms, a severe-weather watch or warning, fog, wind, first frost, frost, bitter cold, first snow, snow, winter storm, ice, heat, after the storm, flood), each with its King James verse checked word for word against two independent KJV texts (`tools/devotion/build_library.py`; the devotions are in `tools/devotion/entries.py`). `devotion/sky.js` picks the sky from the NWS forecast and alerts (tests: `node tools/devotion/test_sky.js`). `devotion.json` is La Porte's pick for today, saved by the "Sky Devotion" job (`scripts/sky_devotion.js`, started by the Timer at 5:20 and 6:20 AM), and it remembers the season's first frost and first snow. `devotion/index.html` is the free box any church or website can show (scoopanddude.github.io/laporteweathernow-posts/devotion/, with ?lat=&lon=&name= for another U.S. town, &from= for "Shared by ...", &theme=dark); on a day with a Weather Service watch or warning it shows the official alert first.
- `sponsor.json` → `weatherSpots` (added Oct. 6, 2026): weather-triggered sponsor spots on the home page. Each one shows, labeled as a sponsor, only on days its weather is in the La Porte forecast (`when`: `freeze`, `cold`, `snow`, `heat`, `wind`, `storms`, `rain`), and never while a warning is in effect for La Porte County. `gameNight`: who presents the Friday night forecasts (laporteweathernow.com/game-night). Both change without a site publish; fill them in like the examples.
- Email alerts: when the daily site check finds something readers would notice (`urgent` in health.json), the job fails on purpose so GitHub emails Scoop.
- Weather radio (added Sept. 30, 2026): every 15 minutes the "Weather radio" job (`scripts/radio_broadcast.py`) writes the La Porte County broadcast from the newest NWS alerts, La Porte airport reading, NWS forecast and today's Daily Scoop, in words made for listening. When the words change, Kokoro-82M (a free, open-source voice, Apache 2.0) records it. Branch `radio` keeps only the newest recording (`laporte-<hash>.mp3`) and `radio.json` (time, length, parts, read-along text); the Alerts page's radio player plays it. Run it by hand from Actions > Weather radio: "force" records even if nothing changed, "check" also runs speech-to-text on the recording and saves the result as `check.txt` on the `radio` branch.
- Daily Video (added Oct. 8, 2026): a short La Porte forecast video for Facebook and Instagram Reels, made every morning at 6:00 AM by the "Daily video" job (`scripts/daily_video.py`, `scripts/video_render.py`, `scripts/video_post.py`) from the NWS forecast, hour-by-hour forecast and La Porte County alerts, read by the weather radio's voice. No video while a short-fused warning is in effect. Branch `video` keeps only the newest video, its cover and `video.json`. Posting needs the `META_TOKEN` secret; YouTube Shorts uploads come from a Google Apps Script (`tools/video/youtube-upload.gs`, setup steps at its top). Setup and rules in `video/README.md`. Tests: `python3 tools/video/test_plan.py`.
- Timer (added Sept. 30, 2026): GitHub's own schedules for this repository have been hours late or missing, so an outside clock (a Google Apps Script trigger, "LPWN timer", in the business Google account) sends a `tick` (repository_dispatch) every 15 minutes. The "Timer" job (`scripts/timer.py`) then starts what's due in La Porte time: the homepage copy, the weather radio, the Daily Scoop updates, the Daily Scoop pages and the snow totals every tick, the NWS snapshot at 5:00, the Daily Video at 6:00 (6:45 as a second chance), the Shore Call at 5:30 (6:30 and 7:30 as second chances), the School Delay Watch record Sunday-Thursday at 8:00 PM (9:00 PM as a second chance), the backup Scoop 6:30-8:09, the site check at 8:10, BLS markets at 9:15 and 4:45, The Chase's map squares on Mondays (3:15 AM, with a 3:15 PM second chance that only builds if the first didn't), and fuel prices Tuesday 5:45 PM and Wednesday and Thursday 9:45 AM. The jobs keep their GitHub schedules as a second chance.
- Guard rails (Sept. 30, 2026):
  - `tools/check_site.py SITE.zip`: run before every publish; it must say PASS (links, version stamps, menus, sitemap, and every page opened in English and Darja on a phone-sized screen with no script errors or sideways scrolling).
  - The daily site check lists `urgent` (readers would notice) and `stuck` (a job stopped) in health.json. Only the daily run started by the timer emails Scoop (it fails on purpose); checks run by hand just report.
  - `tools/lpwn-timer.gs`: the Google Apps Script that sends the timer's ticks and, once a day, emails if a job is stuck. Setup steps are at the top of the file.
  - `tools/newsletter.gs`: the Thursday weekend email (sign-ups to a Brevo list and the Newsletter tab, a welcome email, the weekly send). It goes into the Hard Hat Weather sender Apps Script as a second file; setup steps are at the top.
