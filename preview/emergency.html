Report what you see

If it's safe, tell the National Weather Service about hail, damage, flooding, or a tornado. Reports help forecasters warn the people in the storm's path. How to send a storm report to NWS ↗ (online form, social media, email, or the mPING app). If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 911 first.

AFTER THE STORM

Stay safe, document the damage, and watch for scams

First, stay safe
  • Stay far away from downed power lines and anything touching them. Call 911, then NIPSCO at 1-800-464-7726.
  • Smell gas? Leave right away and call from outside.
  • Run generators outside only, at least 20 feet from windows, doors and vents. Never in a garage, even with the door open. Carbon monoxide can kill in minutes.
  • Power out? Keep the fridge and freezer closed. A closed fridge keeps food safe for about 4 hours; a full freezer about 48 hours (24 if half full).
  • Stay out of flood water. It can hide debris, downed lines and sewage.
Then, for your insurance
  • Take photos and video of all the damage before you clean up or make repairs.
  • Call your insurance company as soon as you can, and write down who you talked to and when.
  • Make only the repairs needed to prevent more damage (a tarp on the roof, boards over a window) and keep the receipts.
  • Need shelter, food or help paying for repairs? Call 211.

Storm-chaser scams: what Indiana's Attorney General warns about

After big storms, out-of-town crews knock on doors. Be skeptical if someone says your roof needs work right away, says they're "doing work down the street," offers a deal for today only, or pressures you to sign.

  • Indiana law requires a written contract for home improvement work over $150, with the price, payment schedule, a detailed description of the work, start and finish dates, and the contractor's name, address and signature.
  • Never pay for the whole job up front. The Attorney General advises a down payment of no more than one-third.
  • Contractors can't offer to pay or waive your insurance deductible, and they can't act as your public adjuster.
  • Get written bids, choose a local, established contractor, and check that they're licensed, bonded and insured with your city or county building department.
  • Report a scam: Indiana Attorney General Consumer Protection Division, 1-800-382-5516 or file a complaint online.

Don't count on any single alert, including this website.
