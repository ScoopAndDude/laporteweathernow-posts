Part 5: Was Belle Gunness still alive in 1931? 🕯️ In Los Angeles, a housekeeper named Esther Carlson was charged with poisoning the 81-year-old man she worked for. She died of tuberculosis before her trial. Two people from La Porte viewed her body and swore it was Belle, and her trunk held a photo of three children said to be Belle's. No one ever proved it. Double-tap ❤️ if you think Belle got away with it.
