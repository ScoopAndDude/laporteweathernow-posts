Seen posts saying a blackout is coming? The Pentagon's FORTRESS America plan is a long-term military plan that starts with bases making their own power. Fox News reports Pentagon documents call for major bases to run 90 days on their own. Nothing announced schedules power cuts for homes.

Still, it's a good question: could your house last 3 days? Part 1, your first hour of an outage: flashlights, not candles. Unplug electronics. Keep the fridge and freezer shut. Report it to your power company (NIPSCO: text OUT to 444111). Stay away from downed power lines. Check on neighbors if it's safe.

FACEBOOK CAPTION: outage-part-2-staying-warm.mp4

Could your house last 3 days? Part 2: staying warm when the power's out. Generators go outside only, at least 20 feet from windows, doors and vents, and never in the garage, even with the door open. Never plug one into a wall outlet. Your CO alarm needs batteries or battery backup, and never heat with a gas oven, stove or grill.

Headache, dizziness, weakness, upset stomach, vomiting, chest pain or confusion, or an alarm going off? Get outside, then call 911. Can't keep warm? In Indiana, call 2-1-1 to find a warming center.

Sources: U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission; CDC; Ready.gov (FEMA); National Weather Service; Indiana Department of Homeland Security; Minnesota State Fire Marshal; Indiana FSSA (Indiana 211).

FACEBOOK CAPTION: outage-part-3-food-and-water.mp4

Could your house last 3 days? Part 3: food and water. A closed fridge keeps food cold about 4 hours, a full freezer about 48 (24 if half full). Fridge without power more than 4 hours? Toss perishables, and when in doubt, throw it out. Store at least 1 gallon of water per person, per day, for at least 3 days, and ask your pharmacist about medicine that needs the fridge.

Save this, and send it to someone who lives alone. 💙
