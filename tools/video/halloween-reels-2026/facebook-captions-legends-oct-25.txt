Tonight's a full moon 🌕 On Orr Lake Road, legend says that's when the doctor walks. The story goes that sometime in the 1900s, a doctor lived out here with his wife and daughter. When they died, he couldn't let them go, and kept their bodies in barrels. On full-moon nights, people say his lantern moves into the woods beside a servant with a hook for a hand. There's no record any of it happened. People live on Orr Lake Road, so if you drive it tonight, keep rolling. Double-tap ❤️ for the next La Porte legend.

2) pine-lake-cemetery-buried-alive.mp4

When La Porte moved its old city cemetery in 1871, a story came up with the coffins ⚰️ Many of the dead were reburied at Pine Lake Cemetery, marked only as unknown. People say one woman was found upside down and twisted in her coffin, as if she'd been buried alive. Pine Lake also holds a mass grave: the bones of about ten unknown men from Belle Gunness's farm, split between two caskets. Visitors report shadows like mist above the stones. Please visit in daylight, and pull into the cemetery rather than stopping on US-35. Double-tap ❤️ for the next La Porte legend.
