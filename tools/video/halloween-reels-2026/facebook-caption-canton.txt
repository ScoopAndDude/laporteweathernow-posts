At Riverview Cemetery in Canton, Georgia, a ghost-tour guide says a toddler on a tour pointed and said "baby," though there was no baby anyone else could see. When they asked its name, he said "Marie." A baby named Marie, who died in 1891, is buried right there. 👻 An hour north, in Fannin County, the Chastain House has stood since the 1850s. The Chastains lost their daughter Alice to diphtheria when she was 10, and in bad weather, neighbors laid out their dead in a room downstairs. Visitors say they still hear children laughing, and little footsteps. If you go, please view the house from the road.
