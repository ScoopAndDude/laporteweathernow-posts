Natural Earth (naturalearthdata.com), public domain: 1:50m land, lakes, country and state lines; 1:10m lakes, state lines and railroads. Cut to the North Atlantic and the Chicago-La Porte area by hand (map/*.geojson). Downloaded Oct. 20, 2026 from github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector.
County map (county-lines, county-fill): U.S. Census Bureau 2010 county boundaries (public domain), as simplified in github.com/plotly/datasets (geojson-counties-fips.json); the lines along the lakeshore were left out and La Porte County's fill runs to the Natural Earth shoreline. county-rivers: Natural Earth 1:10m rivers (public domain).
